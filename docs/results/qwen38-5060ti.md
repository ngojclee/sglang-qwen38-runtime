## Conclusions
1. **2× 5060 Ti cannot reach 200K + >109 tok/s with this stack.** KVarN is the
   only backend that holds 200K; its long-context decode tax caps C1 at ~38 tok/s.
   (Reference: 1×3090 KVarN @112K = 32 tok/s MTP — this config beats it at 200K.)
2. The 109 tok/s figure belongs to the CTX=fast (bf16, ~64K) mode — a different
   context class, not comparable at 200K.
3. 5060 Ti is a 160W card (~448 GB/s) — do not document it as >=100 tok/s.
